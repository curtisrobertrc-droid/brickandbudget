Self Assessment Deadlines UK 2026/27

Key dates for the 2025/26 tax year return, what happens if you miss them, and how to plan for January.

Tax year this return covers

6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026. You file and pay after the year ends.

Key deadlines

Tell HMRC you need a return (if new, or you did not need one last year)5 October 2026
Paper tax return31 October 2026, 11:59pm
Online return if you want the bill collected through your PAYE tax code30 December 2026, 11:59pm
Online tax return AND payment of tax due31 January 2027, 11:59pm
Second payment on account (if you make them)31 July 2027

If you register after 5 October 2026, HMRC will give you a filing deadline 3 months from the date on their letter or email. You must still pay any tax due by 31 January 2027.

Late filing penalties

  • £100 as soon as the return is late, even if no tax is due.
  • After 3 months: £10 a day, up to £900.
  • After 6 months: a further 5% of the tax due or £300, whichever is greater.
  • After 12 months: another 5% or £300.

Late payment penalties

  • 5% of the unpaid tax at 30 days, again at 6 months, and again at 12 months.
  • Interest is also charged on the amount owed.

What to do now

  1. Estimate this year's bill with the take-home pay calculator and set that amount aside each month.
  2. If you work under CIS, check every deduction with the CIS calculator and keep the statements — they reduce what you pay in January.
  3. Read payments on account so the first January bill does not surprise you.
  4. Gather expenses and mileage; see what you can claim.

Frequently asked questions

Do I still get the £100 penalty if I owe no tax?

Yes. The initial late filing penalty applies even when there is no tax to pay.

Can I pay through my tax code?

Often yes if you owe less than £3,000 and file online by 30 December, and you have enough PAYE income. Otherwise pay HMRC directly by 31 January.

What about the 2026/27 tax year?

That year runs 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027. The online return and payment deadline will be 31 January 2028, with a paper deadline of 31 October 2027.
