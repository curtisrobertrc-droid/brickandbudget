Gravel Calculator UK: Tonnes and Bulk Bags

Work out how many tonnes and bulk bags of gravel, sand or MOT Type 1 you need from area and depth.

How to work it out

  1. Measure the length and width in metres and choose a depth in millimetres.
  2. Typical depths: 40 to 50 mm for decorative gravel, 100 mm or more of MOT Type 1 for a driveway sub-base.
  3. Most gravel and aggregates weigh about 1.5 to 1.7 tonnes per m³; 1.6 is a sensible default. Slate and some stone are lighter.
  4. UK bulk (jumbo) bags usually hold around 800 to 850 kg; check your merchant's bag weight.

Worked example

A 5 m by 4 m area at 50 mm: 1 m³, about 1.6 tonnes, so 2 bulk bags.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a bulk bag cover?

At 50 mm deep, a 850 kg bag covers roughly 10 m² of typical gravel.

Should I use a membrane?

Yes, a weed membrane under decorative gravel stops weeds and stops stones sinking into soil.

How much extra should I order?

Add 5% to 10% for compaction and uneven ground, more for sub-base material that compacts.
