Decking Calculator UK: Boards, Joists and Screws

Work out how many deck boards, joists and screws you need for a garden deck of any size.

How to work it out

  1. Measure the deck length (the direction the boards run) and width in metres.
  2. Common UK deck boards are 120 or 144 mm wide, sold in 2.4 m, 3.6 m and 4.8 m lengths. Allow a 5 mm gap between boards for drainage.
  3. Space joists at 400 mm centres for standard softwood boards; 47 x 100 mm or 47 x 150 mm C16/C24 treated timber is typical.
  4. Fix each board with two screws at every joist, and add 10% to boards and joists for cuts.

Worked example

A 4 m by 3 m deck with 144 mm boards: 21 rows of board, 26 boards at 3.6 m, 11 joists needing 8 lengths at 4.8 m, and about 462 screws.

Frequently asked questions

Which way should deck boards run?

Usually across the joists and along the longest dimension, often away from the house so water runs off with the fall.

Do I need planning permission for decking?

Decking is usually permitted development if it is no more than 30 cm above the ground and covers no more than half the garden. Check the Planning Portal.

Grooved side up or down?

Many makers now recommend the smooth side up for grip with modern boards; follow the board manufacturer's guidance.

What screws should I use?

Exterior-grade coated or stainless deck screws, typically 4.5 x 60 mm for 28 to 32 mm boards.
