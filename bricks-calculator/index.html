Brick Calculator UK: Bricks and Mortar

Calculate how many bricks and how much mortar you need for a wall.

How to work it out

  1. UK standard bricks are 215 x 102.5 x 65 mm, laid with 10 mm joints.
  2. That gives 60 bricks per m² for a single skin (half-brick wall).
  3. A full-brick (double skin) wall needs 120 per m².
  4. Add 5% for cuts and breakages.

Worked example

A 5 m long, 1 m high single-skin garden wall: 5 m² x 60 = 300 bricks, plus 5% = 315 bricks and about 0.11 m³ of mortar.

Frequently asked questions

What mortar mix?

Commonly 1:4 to 1:5 cement to building sand for general brickwork; follow structural guidance for load-bearing walls.

Do I need foundations?

Yes. Even a small garden wall needs a concrete footing; taller or retaining walls need professional design.

Do I need planning permission?

Walls over 1 m next to a highway, or over 2 m elsewhere, usually need permission. Check the Planning Portal.
