About Brick & Budget

Brick & Budget is a set of free calculators and guides for UK homeowners and tradespeople, built by people with hands-on experience in the North East building and refurbishment trade.

Every calculator uses UK product sizes and is checked against manufacturer guidance. Results are estimates: site conditions, waste and product variation all matter, so confirm with your supplier before ordering.
