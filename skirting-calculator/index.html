Skirting Board Calculator

Work out how many lengths of skirting board or coving you need for a room.

How to work it out

  1. Measure the room perimeter: add the length of every wall.
  2. Deduct the width of each doorway.
  3. Skirting is commonly sold in 2.4 m, 3 m, 4.2 m and 4.4 m lengths. Longer lengths mean fewer joints.
  4. Add 10% for mitres and offcuts.

Worked example

A 14 m perimeter with one 0.85 m doorway: 13.15 m of run, plus 10% at 2.4 m lengths is 7 lengths.

Frequently asked questions

MDF or timber?

Primed MDF is cheaper, stable and ready to paint; timber suits staining and high-impact areas.

How do I join internal corners?

Scribing gives a tighter internal joint than a mitre; external corners are mitred.

Does coving use the same calculation?

Yes, measure the ceiling perimeter. No doorway deduction is needed.
