How to work it out

  1. Measure wall area: total wall width multiplied by height. Ignore small windows; deduct large patio doors.
  2. Check the coverage on the tin, commonly 10 to 14 m² per litre for emulsion.
  3. Plan two coats on most walls, and a mist coat plus two coats on new plaster.
  4. Dark-to-light colour changes may need a third coat.

Worked example

30 m² of wall, two coats at 12 m² per litre: 30 x 2 / 12 = 5 litres, so one 5 L tin or two 2.5 L tins.

Frequently asked questions

Does ceiling paint cover the same?

Similar, but ceilings often need a little more because of absorbent surfaces.

Should I buy trade paint?

Trade emulsions often have better opacity and coverage, but check the coverage figure on the tin.

How much for woodwork?

Gloss and satinwood cover around 12 to 16 m² per litre; a 750 ml tin does most rooms' skirting and doors.
