How to work it out

  1. Measure the loft floor area (length x width) in m².
  2. The recommended depth for mineral wool loft insulation in the UK is 270 mm.
  3. Lay the first layer between the joists (typically 100 mm), then a second layer across the joists to make up the depth, usually 170 mm.
  4. Roll coverage varies by thickness and brand: around 8.3 m² for a 100 mm roll and 5.6 m² for 170 mm is typical. Check the pack label.

Worked example

A 40 m² loft with no insulation: 6 rolls of 100 mm between the joists plus 8 rolls of 170 mm across them, 14 rolls in total.

Frequently asked questions

Is 270 mm enough?

270 mm of mineral wool is the usual recommendation and meets current guidance for existing homes.

Can I board over insulation?

Not directly over 270 mm without crushing it; use loft legs to raise the boards above the insulation.

Do I need to leave a gap at the eaves?

Yes, keep the eaves clear for ventilation to avoid condensation in the roof space.

What safety kit do I need?

Gloves, long sleeves, goggles and a dust mask. Only walk on the joists or boards.
