Plasterboard Calculator UK

How many sheets of plasterboard do you need? Work out boards, screws and tape for any wall or ceiling.

How to work it out

  1. Measure the length and height of each wall (or the length and width of a ceiling) in metres.
  2. Deduct doors and windows: a standard internal door is about 1.9 m² (0.84 x 2.04 m).
  3. Standard UK boards are 2400 x 1200 mm, covering 2.88 m². Add 10% for offcuts, or 15% for rooms with lots of corners.
  4. Use 12.5 mm board for walls and ceilings; 15 mm or acoustic board where sound matters, and moisture-resistant board in bathrooms.

Worked example

A 4 m by 2.4 m wall with one door: 9.6 m² minus 1.9 m² is 7.7 m². Add 10% and divide by 2.88 m², giving 3 sheets.

Frequently asked questions

Should I buy 2400 or 1800 mm boards?

2400 mm boards mean fewer joints on standard 2.4 m ceilings. 1800 mm boards are easier to carry and fit in a car.

Screw spacing?

Typically every 230 mm on walls and 150 mm on ceilings for 12.5 mm board; check the board maker's guidance.

Do I need to skim afterwards?

Either tape and joint for a decorative finish, or skim with a finishing plaster such as multi-finish.
