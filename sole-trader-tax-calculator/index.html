Sole Trader Take-Home Pay Calculator UK 2026/27

Estimate your Income Tax, Class 4 National Insurance and how much to set aside each month. England, Wales and Northern Ireland rates for 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027.

Rates used (2026/27)

  • Personal Allowance £12,570, reduced by £1 for every £2 of adjusted net income over £100,000, reaching zero at £125,140.
  • Basic rate 20% on taxable income from £12,571 to £50,270; higher rate 40% from £50,271 to £125,140; additional rate 45% above £125,140.
  • Class 4 National Insurance: 6% on profits from £12,570 to £50,270, and 2% above £50,270.
  • Class 2 National Insurance: not payable. With profits of £7,105 or more, it is treated as paid to protect your record. Below £7,105 you can choose to pay voluntarily at £3.65 a week.
  • Simplified mileage for cars and vans: 55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles in 2026/27 (up from 45p before 6 April 2026), then 25p a mile.

How it works

Profit is turnover minus allowable expenses minus the mileage allowance. Income Tax on the profit is the tax on your total income minus the tax on your other income alone, so PAYE wages use up your allowance and bands first. Class 4 is worked out on business profit only. You cannot use flat-rate mileage for a vehicle you have claimed capital allowances on.

Worked examples (no other income, no mileage)

  • £20,000 profit: Income Tax £1,486, Class 4 £445.80, take-home £18,068.20.
  • £60,000 profit: Income Tax £11,432, Class 4 £2,456.60, take-home £46,111.40.
  • £120,000 profit: Personal Allowance tapered to £2,570, Income Tax £39,432, Class 4 £3,656.60, take-home £76,911.40.
