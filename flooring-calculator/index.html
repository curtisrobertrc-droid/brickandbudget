Laminate Flooring Calculator UK

Work out how many packs of laminate or engineered flooring, underlay and beading you need.

How to work it out

  1. Measure the room length and width in metres. For L-shaped rooms, split into rectangles and add.
  2. Check the m² per pack printed on the box.
  3. Add 5% to 10% for cuts; 15% for herringbone.
  4. Underlay is sold by m²; beading or scotia is sold by length to cover the perimeter.

Worked example

A 4 m by 3.5 m room: 14 m² plus 8% = 15.1 m², at 2.2 m² per pack that is 7 packs.

Frequently asked questions

Should I acclimatise flooring?

Most makers recommend leaving packs flat in the room for 48 hours before fitting.

Expansion gap?

Leave about 8 to 10 mm around the edges, covered by skirting or beading.

Which way should boards run?

Usually parallel to the main light source or the longest wall.
