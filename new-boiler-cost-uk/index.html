How Much Does a New Boiler Cost in the UK? (2026)

A like-for-like combi boiler replacement typically costs £2,500 to £4,000 supplied and fitted in 2026. Changing from a system or conventional boiler to a combi, or moving the boiler, usually costs more.

Typical 2026 price ranges (supplied and fitted)

Like-for-like combi replacement£2,500 to £4,000
Average gas boiler jobabout £3,000
System or conventional boiler, like-for-likeabout £3,000
System or conventional converted to combiabout £4,000
Boiler supply only (combi / system / conventional)about £1,500 average
Installation labour£1,000 to £2,000

Figures are national averages from Checkatrade (updated October 2026). London and the South East tend to be higher; the North East lower. Biomass systems are a different price band entirely (often £10,000+ for the unit alone).

How to keep the cost down

  • Keep the new boiler in the same place with the same flue route where you can.
  • Get at least three Gas Safe quotes that list the boiler model, flue work, filter, controls, disposal of the old boiler and any powerflush.
  • Ask about manufacturer warranties (often 5 to 10 years when fitted by an accredited installer).
  • Check whether you are eligible for any current energy-efficiency schemes on GOV.UK before you pay full price.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a like-for-like combi replacement?

Checkatrade puts a like-for-like combi replacement at about £2,500 to £4,000 supplied and fitted in 2026, with a typical gas boiler job averaging around £3,000.

How much is installation alone?

Published guides put boiler installation labour at roughly £1,000 to £2,000, depending on access, flue work and whether the system is being changed.

Do I need a Gas Safe engineer?

Yes. Any gas boiler must be fitted by a Gas Safe registered engineer. Ask for their Gas Safe licence number and check it on the Gas Safe Register.

How long does a simple swap take?

A straightforward like-for-like combi replacement is often done in a day (about 4 to 6 hours). Changing boiler type or moving the boiler usually takes longer.

Is VAT included?

Homeowner price guides are usually quoted including VAT. Always ask whether a quote is VAT-inclusive.
