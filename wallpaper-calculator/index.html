Wallpaper Calculator UK: How Many Rolls?

Work out how many rolls of wallpaper you need, including pattern repeat.

How to work it out

  1. Measure the total width of the walls to paper and the wall height.
  2. Standard UK rolls are 10.05 m long and 53 cm wide.
  3. Each drop is the wall height plus about 10 cm trimming, plus the pattern repeat.
  4. Divide roll length by drop length to get full drops per roll, then divide the number of drops needed.

Worked example

14 m of wall at 2.4 m high, no repeat: 27 drops are needed, each roll gives 4 full drops, so 7 rolls.

Frequently asked questions

Should I buy an extra roll?

Yes, and check all rolls share the same batch number so the shade matches.

What about wide rolls?

Change the roll width and length to match the label, for example 70 cm or 1 m wide.

Do I deduct windows?

Not for small windows; the offcuts are rarely usable. Deduct large patio doors.
