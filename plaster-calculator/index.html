Plaster Calculator: How Much Plaster Do I Need?

Work out how many 25 kg bags of finishing plaster you need for a skim coat, by area and thickness.

How to work it out

  1. Measure the area in m² (length x height for each wall).
  2. A skim coat of finishing plaster is usually 2 mm, applied in two passes.
  3. Multi-finish covers roughly 10 to 11 m² per 25 kg bag at 2 mm, which is about 1.15 kg per m² per mm.
  4. Add 10% for mixing waste and the first bucket that goes off.

Worked example

A 12 m² wall at 2 mm: 12 x 2 x 1.15 = 27.6 kg, plus 10% is 30.4 kg, so 2 bags.

Frequently asked questions

How long does plaster take to dry?

Typically 3 to 7 days for a skim, longer in cold or damp rooms. It is dry when the colour is an even light pink.

Can I paint new plaster straight away?

Seal it first with a mist coat of watered-down emulsion or a dedicated plaster primer.

Bonding or multi-finish?

Bonding is an undercoat for building up depth; multi-finish is the final skim coat.
