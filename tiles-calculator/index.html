Tile Calculator UK: Tiles, Adhesive and Grout

Calculate how many tiles you need, plus adhesive and grout, for floors and walls.

How to work it out

  1. Measure each wall or floor area in m² and add them together.
  2. Enter the tile size in millimetres.
  3. Add 10% waste for straight lay, 15% for diagonal or herringbone patterns.
  4. Adhesive use depends on trowel size: about 3 to 5 kg per m² is typical for standard tiles.

Worked example

6 m² with 300 x 600 mm tiles (0.18 m² each): 6 x 1.1 / 0.18 = 36.7, so 37 tiles, about 24 kg adhesive and 3 kg grout.

Frequently asked questions

Should I buy extra tiles?

Yes. Keep a few spare from the same batch in case one cracks later; batches can vary in shade.

Which adhesive for large tiles?

Use a flexible, rapid or large-format adhesive and a bigger notched trowel, following the tile maker's guidance.

Do I need to tank a shower?

Yes, wet areas should be tanked with a waterproofing system before tiling.
