Fence Panel and Post Calculator UK

Work out how many 6ft fence panels, posts and bags of postcrete you need for a fence run.

How to work it out

  1. Measure the total fence run in metres.
  2. A standard UK 6ft panel is 1.83 m wide. Each panel needs one post, plus one extra post at the end of every separate run.
  3. Use 100 x 100 mm (4 x 4 in) posts, about 2.4 m long for a 1.8 m fence, with roughly a quarter of the post in the ground.
  4. Allow two 20 kg bags of postcrete per post in typical soil, or one in firm ground.

Worked example

A 12 m straight run: 12 / 1.83 = 6.6, so 7 panels, 8 posts and 16 bags of postcrete.

Frequently asked questions

How deep should fence posts go?

About 600 mm for a 1.8 m fence, or a quarter of the post length.

Timber or concrete posts?

Concrete posts last longer and suit slotted panels; treated timber posts are cheaper and easier to cut.

How high can a garden fence be?

Up to 2 m without planning permission, or 1 m next to a highway. Check the Planning Portal.

Do I need gravel boards?

They keep the panel off the soil and stop it rotting; most fitters use one per panel.
