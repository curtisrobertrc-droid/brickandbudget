Concrete Calculator UK

Work out the volume of concrete and the number of ready-mix bags for slabs, bases and paths.

How to work it out

  1. Measure length and width in metres and depth in millimetres.
  2. Typical depths: 100 mm for a shed base or path, 150 mm for a driveway or garage floor.
  3. Add 5% to 10% for uneven ground.
  4. A 20 kg bag of ready-mix makes roughly 0.01 m³. For larger jobs, ready-mixed concrete delivered by truck is usually cheaper above about 1 m³.

Worked example

A 3 m by 2 m shed base at 100 mm: 0.6 m³ plus 5% is 0.63 m³, about 64 bags of ready-mix, or around 8 bags of cement in a 1:2:4 mix.

Frequently asked questions

What mix for a shed base?

A general-purpose 1:2:4 mix (cement:sand:aggregate) or a C20 ready-mix is common.

Do I need a sub-base?

Usually yes: about 75 to 100 mm of compacted hardcore (MOT Type 1) under the slab.

How long before I can build on it?

Light use after about 48 hours; concrete reaches most of its strength at 28 days.
