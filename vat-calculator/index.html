VAT Calculator UK: Add or Remove 20% VAT

Add VAT to a net price or remove VAT from a gross price, at 20%, 5% or any rate.

How to work it out

  1. Standard UK VAT is 20%; the reduced rate is 5% (for example some energy-saving materials); some items are zero-rated.
  2. To add VAT, multiply the net amount by 1.2.
  3. To remove VAT, divide the gross amount by 1.2. Do not just subtract 20%.
  4. Enter 1 to add VAT or 2 to remove VAT.

Worked example

£120 including VAT: £120 / 1.2 = £100 net, so the VAT is £20. Subtracting 20% would wrongly give £96.

Frequently asked questions

Do builders charge VAT?

Only if VAT registered. The registration threshold is £90,000 of taxable turnover.

What is the domestic reverse charge?

For some construction services between VAT-registered businesses in CIS, the customer accounts for the VAT instead of the supplier.

Is VAT charged on new builds?

Construction of new dwellings is generally zero-rated; most renovation work is standard-rated.
